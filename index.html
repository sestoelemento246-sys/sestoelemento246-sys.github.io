BONKA Auto Poster

BONKA Auto Poster is an internal tool used by BONKA Official to schedule and publish its own product videos to its own TikTok account using the TikTok Content Posting API.

How it works:

  1. The account owner signs in with TikTok and authorizes the app.
  2. The owner prepares videos, captions and a posting schedule.
  3. At the scheduled time, the tool uploads the video to the authorized TikTok account.
