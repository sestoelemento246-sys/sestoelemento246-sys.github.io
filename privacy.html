Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BONKA Auto Poster (the "Service"), operated by BONKA Official, handles information when you use the Service.

1. Information we collect

  • TikTok account information: when you sign in with TikTok we receive basic profile information (such as your open ID, display name and avatar) through the scopes you approve.
  • Access tokens: TikTok issues access and refresh tokens so the Service can publish videos on your behalf.
  • Content you provide: the videos, captions and schedule you choose to publish.

2. How we use information

We use this information only to authenticate you and to publish the videos you schedule to your own TikTok account. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it.

3. Storage and security

Tokens and scheduled content are stored on the device or server operated by BONKA Official that runs the Service, and are protected from unauthorized access. They are not shared with third parties other than TikTok, which receives the content you publish.

4. Retention and deletion

We keep tokens only while you use the Service. You can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services), after which the tokens stop working. You can ask us to delete any stored data by contacting us.

5. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country).

6. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The latest version will always be available on this page.
